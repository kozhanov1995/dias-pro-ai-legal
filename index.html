DIAS PRO AI Studio

Operator: Dias Kozhanov · Almaty, Kazakhstan

DIAS PRO AI Studio is a private publishing tool used by one creator, the owner of the DIAS PRO AI channel, to prepare short videos about AI tools and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts.

The app assembles vertical videos and image carousels and, only after the owner approves each post, publishes them to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Telegram. It also reads performance statistics of those same accounts so the owner can see which content works.

The app is not offered to the public. It has a single user: the account owner.
