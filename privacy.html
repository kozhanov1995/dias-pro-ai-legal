Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy describes how DIAS PRO AI Studio (“the app”), a private tool operated by Dias Kozhanov, Almaty, Kazakhstan, handles data. The app has a single user: its owner.

What data the app accesses

  • Access tokens for the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Telegram accounts, obtained through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth) or official bot API.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (account name and ID).
  • Statistics of the owner's own posts: views, likes, comments, saves, shares, watch time.
  • Public information about public creator accounts, used only to research content ideas, as allowed by each platform's API.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos and carousels the owner has approved, to the owner's own accounts.
  • To show the owner statistics about the owner's own content.

The data is not sold, not shared with or transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the app's private server and are never written to logs or messages. Statistics are stored as aggregated numbers per post. Access to the app is limited to the owner.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of each platform:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services;
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites;
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions;
  • Telegram: remove or block the bot.

After revocation the stored tokens no longer work and are deleted. Deletion of any stored data can also be requested through the contact below.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
